# CLD-0201: Repeated failed sign-ins: jt-admin@cloudora.io

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 1 · 2026-08-24
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
Nine failed sign-in attempts were detected on the newly created privileged account IT-admin between 06:48 and 07:09 UTC. The attempts were from the London office using a corporate Windows 11 device, and there was no successful login when the alert triggered.

## Hypothesis
If malicious, someone is bruteforcing a freshly created admin account. If benign, the new starter is fighting his temporary password on day one.

## Evidence checked
I checked the sign-in logs and helpdesk records. All attempts came from the corporate office and the same Windows 11 environment. James had contacted the helpdesk about an issue with his temporary password. After a verified password reset, he successfully signed in at 07:22

## Action
The alert did its job, it fired on something that look suspicious, but the activity behind it is benign. It's explained, it's authorized and it's resolved.

## Justification
Every alert came from the office egress on a corporate Windows 11 build. HD-5121 logs. Turner phoning the helpdesk about caps lock with his temporary password, and sign-in log shows a first-try success at 07:22 after a verified reset. An attacker does not phone the helpdesk from inside the building.

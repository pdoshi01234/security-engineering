# CLD-0204: Internal port sweep outside scan window: 203.0.113.44

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 1 · 2026-08-24
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
The vulnerability scanner LDN-SCAN-01 scanned more than 3,800 ports across 160 internal hosts outside its normal scanning schedule. The scan happened Sunday night instead of the usual Monday night schedule.

## Hypothesis
If malicious, then someone else is using the scanner's address or the scanner itself has been compromised. If benign, then the vulnerability scan schedule was officially changed for this week.

## Evidence checked
 I checked change record CHG-2101, which confirms that the scan was moved to Sunday 23:00 through Monday 03:00 for this week. The alert occurred within this approved window. The traffic pattern has also matched normal scanner behavior with sequential SYN probes and no data transfer.

## Action
 Close the alert as a false positive and reference change CHG-2101.

## Justification
The scan looked unusual because it happened outside the normal schedule, but CHG-2101 confirms that the schedule was temporarily changed. The scan happened within the approved window and the traffic pattern has also matched the normal behavior of the vulnerability scanner.
